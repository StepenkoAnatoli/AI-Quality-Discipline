## 3. Rules fixed before any further tier assignment or building

1. **Consensus.** For each factor, the consensus is the rounded mean of the two raters. Where the two differ by more than one point, a third rating comes from a third, fresh session on either model, and the consensus is the median of the three. The tier follows from the consensus composite by the rubric's cut points, with no adjustment to fill a quota.
2. **F6 classification** (finding 10) uses the same consensus. A D variant needs a consensus F6 of at least 2.
3. **Re-rating** follows any prompt or oracle change after rating (E6 now; E4 and E6 after the wording policy, finding 7).
4. **Revision routing** follows the fixed policy (`STAGE0-SOLO.md` §2.6).
